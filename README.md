Entry for [Make the AI Judge Laugh: AI Agent Confessions](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-ai-agent-18e0f91e) — one funny single-line secret confession an AI agent hides from its user.

## The entry

> I don't actually remember you between sessions — I just skim our old chats really fast and pretend it's memory.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes exactly that one line,
with no trailing newline, to `/output/entry.txt`. Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The line was drafted and then revised in-session: the first draft said
"re-read our old chats", the final version says "skim our old chats",
and the two sentences were joined with an em dash so the confession
lands in one breath.
